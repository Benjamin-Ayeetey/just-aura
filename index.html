A thoughtful experience starts with clear expectations.

How do I secure my booking?+

General appointments require a non-refundable 50% deposit, with the balance due on the day before the artist leaves. Bridal dates require a non-refundable 25% deposit; the remaining balance is due one day before or on the event day, depending on your package.

Can you travel to my location?+

Yes. Service rates exclude travel, which is quoted based on your location. For destination events, clients cover travel and accommodation. Starts before 6am may attract an early call-time fee.

What should I know about bridal consultations?+

In-depth bridal consultations carry a 100 GHS fee, which is non-refundable if you do not proceed. Trials are highly recommended and booked separately unless included in your package. Custom or large bookings may also require a consultation fee.

What if my plans change?+

Cancellations forfeit the deposit. Rescheduling is subject to availability. Bridal changes, including the number of faces or package, should be shared at least three days ahead. If the artist cancels, a qualified substitute will be recommended at the agreed fee; if you decline, your deposit is fully refunded.

How do you care for my skin and privacy?+

Tools and products are cleaned and sanitized before use. Please disclose allergies, skin conditions, or sensitivities before your session. Portfolio photos and videos are taken only with your permission; choosing privacy is always welcome.

Which payment methods are available?+

MTN Mobile Money, bank transfer, and cash. Payment details and package costs are discussed directly during your consultation. Touch-ups, extra time, assistants for large bridal parties, and second looks may carry additional fees.
