# Just Aura — art direction

Visual thesis: beauty with presence. Forest green (#002400), olive (#58631E), sage (#BACF8C), and a pale sage neutral. Brand gradients become broad tonal chapter changes, not decorative blobs. Montserrat is the brand's text and UI family; Cormorant Garamond is an editorial display companion. Austin Signature is not supplied as a licensed font file, so it is not imitated. The widely spaced text-logo variation is used as a semantic wordmark.

Hero: oversized statement with a close crop of supplied pic2, framed by forest green. Sequence: opening / selected looks / philosophy / services / experience / booking policies / contact. Motion: composed introduction, word reveals, shallow photo parallax, desktop lookbook pin and horizontal progression, section transitions. Lenis selected over Locomotive for native document flow and direct GSAP ticker integration. No Three.js: photography is the focal material.

All nine photographs are user-supplied from assets; originals remain unchanged. Optimized WebP derivatives are generated for delivery. No reference media, testimonials, awards, or partnerships are used. Reference studied: https://lagencedesignstudio.com/ for hierarchy, imagery pacing, and scroll rhythm only.

Contacts are sample configuration requested by the user. Booking prepares a downloadable enquiry, does not send, and does not confirm availability or collect payment. Replace sample contacts in src/config.js before launch.
